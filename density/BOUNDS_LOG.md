## Closed

Five iterations, five reviews. Iterations 1–4 each removed a modeling error that changed a number: the drive as a resource on both sides, the hot-set double count, the endurance term, the whole-memory rung's drive accounting. Review 5 found qualifications only. The conclusions that survived every review: at technique parity the bound is 1.0–1.1× on 128 cores and 1.8–2.0× (no DAX) or 1.25–1.4× (DAX) on 256, times an unmeasured kernel-path ratio; 5–10× exists only against rungs that keep idle sandboxes resident or rewrite whole memory per cycle; the composed scheme is at 91–100 % of the bound on 128 cores at 500 ms and 5 s, 73–89 % on 256 at 500 ms, 62–68 % at 50 ms; the candidate space is covered term by term, with C15 (tiered idle state, conditional) the one addition the bound revealed.
