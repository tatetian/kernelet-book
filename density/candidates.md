# Candidate optimizations: the global list

Every optimization considered for the density scheme, adopted or not, one line each. Details, evidence and raw data are in `candidates/<id>/`. "K" means the candidate depends on something only kernelets have (one address space, host-owned frames, a memory-safe kernel, the same source on both sides); "G" means a microVM stack can do it too, in which case the gain counts as a relative advantage only against platforms that do not. Gains are per-sandbox DRAM changes against the baseline of `benchmark/README.md`. Evidence grades: **M** measured here, **L** published figure cited, **A** analytic derivation with stated assumptions, **U** unverified (does not count). Every candidate was reviewed by a skeptical reviewer; the status column says what survived.

| id | candidate | K/G | acts on | gain alone | evidence | isolation effect | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | [Shared image pages](candidates/C01-shared-file-pages/): whole image grains from a host arena mapped read-only into the kernelet window; no guest copy of public image content | G for the saving (DAX-capable VM stacks and containers have it; Firecracker's virtio-pmem DAX exists since v1.14, Kata ships DAX off); K for the cost: no EPT, no DAX window | `m_file(public)` | `0.98 × F_public`: 376 → 180 MiB per warm sandbox at `F_public = 200` (2.1×) | M, L | container grade: Flush+Reload on shared read-only pages, no content inference; per-sandbox opt-out | adopted, revised |
| C02 | [Cooperative grain return](candidates/C02-cooperative-memory/): a kernelet returns free grains through a call; a shrink job asks it to reclaim | K in mechanism; the saving is what free-page reporting gives a VM | `m_kproc` | 0–5 MiB; the prerequisite of C03 and C05; needs a reclaim subsystem the kernel proper lacks | A, U | none, given the unmap-and-flush path | adopted as prerequisite, revised |
| C03 | [Eviction of idle kernelets](candidates/C03-host-paging/): cooperative swap-out to NVMe or the compressed pool, grain return, recorded-working-set prefetch on wake | G (host swap, suspend to a snapshot; Fly.io resumes suspended VMs in a few hundred ms) | idle `P_proc`, `F_written`, `m_kproc` | idle 167 → 26–31 MiB at 500 ms (hot set 24 MiB, REAP's average, plus the kernel state a guest-side reclaimer cannot evict), ~2 MiB at 5 s with C13's hibernation (7 MiB without) | M, L, A | none, given C02's unmap-and-flush and a root-wide flush at release; the host-transparent first draft was an isolation break and is withdrawn | adopted, redesigned; requires C02 |
| C04 | [Lean fixed cost](candidates/C04-lean-fixed-cost/): 4 KiB data pages, fully backed 64–96 KiB stacks, pools sized to one vCPU | K | `m_fixed` | Blueprint's 9–13 MiB host-side → ~2 MiB, estimated; parity with a restored VM's 6–10 MiB | A | none (the demand-backed stack of the first draft is dropped as infeasible) | adopted, revised |
| C05 | [Templates](candidates/C05-template-clone/): a process-level checkpoint of the initialized runtime, restored copy-on-write inside a fresh kernelet; kernel-level clone deferred to C13 | G (snapshot restore) | initial `P_proc` | same sharing as a snapshot restore; not a gain over the baseline | M, L | container grade, as C01 | adopted in the process-level form; kernel-level clone withdrawn as infeasible without C13 |
| C06 | [Idle CPU cost](candidates/C06-idle-cpu/): no vCPU thread, no idle tick | K | CPU | ≤ 3 cores per 10,000 idle sandboxes (VM measured at 0.033 % of a core idle with a resident Node.js) | M | none | adopted as a Blueprint correction; below the model's resolution |
| C07 | [Compressed idle memory](candidates/C07-compression/): the compressed pool as the swap target at the 50 ms tier | G (`zswap`) | idle private state | 2.0× (LZ4, from a 2.64× `zlib` measurement on mixed pages); costs 2–6 cores at 10,000 sandboxes | M, L | none | adopted for the 50 ms tier, revised |
| C08 | Far memory (CXL, RDMA) for cold pages | G | idle DRAM | hardware not common; not counted; C13 makes it possible later | U | none | deferred |
| C09 | Content-based deduplication (KSM-like) across tenants | G | all | 2–3× on unikernel-style images in the literature | L | **weakens**: content inference by write timing | rejected by the isolation floor |
| C10 | Sharing written file pages across tenants | G | `m_file` | small | A | weakens: tenant-private content | rejected |
| C11 | Smaller guest kernel structures (slab sizes, pools for one vCPU) | K | `m_kproc` | 2–5 MiB | A | none | folded into C04 |
| C12 | One virtual CPU per agent sandbox unless asked | G | `m_fixed`, CPU | avoids per-vCPU replicas, workers and pools | A | none | folded into C04 |
| C13 | [Relocatable kernelet memory](candidates/C13-relocatable-memory/): kernelet-physical addresses with a host-written p2m, translated in vOSTD's PTE codec; the m2p carried in each entry's ignored bits | K (what the EPT gives a VM, without the second-level walk) | enables hibernation (5 s tier), the kernel-level clone, C08; bounds C03's page tables | none by itself; 3–4 decodes and one p2m lookup per fault, 1–5 % of a fault | A, L | unchanged for the kernel proper; a vOSTD bug's bad address is caught by the p2m bound | adopted as enabler, major revision; D13's naming with D58's mapping |
| C14 | [CPU per operation](candidates/C14-cpu-per-operation/): no exit and no second zeroing on first touch | K | `c_active` | first-touch faults 2.15× cheaper than a non-hugepage VM (measured); 1.1–1.3× on the CPU-bound density for fault-heavy agents against such VMs, 1.0–1.1× at parity [fraction unverified; I/O term dropped] | M, U | none | adopted as a sensitivity, revised |
| C15 | Tiered idle state at the 50 ms tier: the recorded hot set compressed in DRAM, the rest on NVMe, misses paid at ~90 µs from the drive, a budget of about 400–500 pages per wake at 50 ms in the mean; identified by the bounds analysis (`bounds.md`, §2.2) | G (`zswap` with writeback) | idle private state at 50 ms | 99 → ~19 MiB idle (kernelet), 105–203 → ~18 (VM); moves both sides to the CPU bound on 128 cores | A, U | none (C07's pool, C03's device) | listed, not composed: conditional on the record missing at most a few hundred pages per wake, which is unmeasured and least likely at this tier |

Composition is in `README.md`; the upper bound every candidate is measured against is in `bounds.md`.
