# Staged plan

**Milestone 0 (weeks): the fixes worth making regardless, and the checks.** Per-process oops budget that never escalates to abort; the shim's deallocation error routed away from `poweroff`; the transitive dependency check, the link-time section audit with per-target baselines, and the TCB budget check in CI over the OSDK artifact; a `--no-default-features` build and a backtrace-free panic path measured.

**Milestone 1 (months): two kernelets, one workload, on ramfs.** The manifest trick and the facade's identical/virtualized/absent lists with the executable allowlist test; `aster-kernelet` under `forbid(unsafe_code)` with the two library crates against the facade and the relocation of the host subsystems; the linker-script window sections and the per-kernelet kernel page table; `Task` as a host object with `ThreadRef`, the register state, the name-based `Waker`, the two guardians with the lock depth, the tick-path abandon and both tick budgets, the stack check; the arena in the heap window with magazines, both bands, reservation-first frames and the death reserve; T1 at the trampoline, per job, and at the page-fault entry with `#[kernelet_drop]`; the worker and `reenter` by name; `MemArena`, `CpuBudget`, `Timer`, and `VirtioDev` for console and rng only; two kernelets each running a package install and a compile on a ramfs root, with an adversarial neighbor, the create/destroy loop with leak assertions, and the CR3-write and window-flush measurement of [§7.2](../limitations/todos.md) item 1. **Not H4-complete** (no kernel preemption) and **not the storage path**.

**Milestone 2 (months): H4 in full and the storage path.** Kernel preemption with the depth-based throttling exemption; T2 in thread context with counter restoration; the drain list implemented and audited, including deactivation, the cursor's lists and the RRef rows; the frame-reset primitive and the owner sweep; virtio-blk with a per-kernelet ext2, the page-cache frame state out of `MetaSlot`, the `xarray` rewrite and the shrinker; fault injection (panic, OOM, kernel-mode spin, forced kill, a panic inside a kernel-mode fault, a stack overflow) against a real workload next door; the false-sharing microbenchmark; the page-table diff test.

**Milestone 3 (months): network, vsock, passthrough, and the numbers.** virtio-net with the host demultiplexer; virtio-vsock over `RRef` with credit, against the booted prototype's numbers; `Passthrough` under the IOMMU; I/O accounting; syscall latency and a syscall-heavy macro-benchmark against Docker, gVisor and Firecracker, publishing losses as well as wins; kernelet fork from the two windows for kernelets at the syscall boundary, measured against Firecracker's snapshot restore.

**Then, and only then**, a public time-boxed escape contest against a reproducible image.
