# The trusted base

Shared by every kernelet, and therefore the TCB: OSTD (45,600 lines, about 6,000 proven); the physical drivers (39,341 lines in sixteen components, of which about 28,900 are linked); the two allocators (2,100); the relocated subsystems (about 13,000); the facade and the endovisor, now including the virtio backends and the RRef table (estimated 8,000–12,000, up from Alternative A's 6,000–10,000 because the backends are real code, though the ten request kinds and the `aster-block` rewrite are gone); and, unless removed, the third-party crates a reachability measurement of the linked image put at about 600,000 lines, 92% of it two crates (`iced-x86` under the default confidential-VM feature, `gimli` for backtraces) that two build levers remove. Against that base, the measured reachable fraction from kernelet code was 92.5% of function bytes; the argument for the design is not that the base is small but that every shared item is immutable, handle-mediated with per-kernelet accounting, or never entered with a kernelet callback.
